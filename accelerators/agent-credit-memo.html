How the Agent Works

The Credit Memo Agent follows a structured reasoning chain — the same analytical process a senior credit analyst would follow, but executed in seconds with full auditability.

1. Data Ingestion

Ingests annual reports, financial statements, and industry data. Extracts structured data from unstructured documents.

2. Ratio Analysis

Computes 12+ financial ratios — liquidity, leverage, profitability, coverage — and benchmarks against sector peers.

3. Risk Assessment

Identifies credit risks — concentration, covenant triggers, cash flow adequacy, market conditions — with severity ratings.

4. Memo Generation

Produces a structured credit memorandum with recommendation, conditions, and covenant proposals — ready for credit committee.
