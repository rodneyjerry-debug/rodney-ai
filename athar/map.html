Object model

Everything hangs off one record.

The AI system is the centre. Assessments version rather than overwrite, decisions chain rather than update, and evidence attaches to control instances rather than floating free. That shape is what makes the history defensible.

Two more sit behind these: control and tier_rule, which are content-pack rows rather than tenant data — they arrive with a content release and every tenant records the version it is on. That separation is what lets a regulatory change ship without a code release, and one engine serve banking, telecom and healthcare from different packs.

Release bands

Three releases, three buyers.

The wedge is deliberately narrow. Each band earns the right to the next by holding real data the next band needs.

Methodology

Where the twelve modules went.

The operating model was never the deliverable — it is what the software computes. Each methodology module becomes data models, forms, gates and computed fields, or it stays advisory and out of the platform.

ModuleBecomesBand

M09 Talent is the one module that does not become software. Skills ladders, Saudization planning and adoption mechanics are advisory work — putting them in the platform would produce a feature nobody opens. Naming that honestly is better than shipping a module to complete a set.
