# Agent-Washing — LinkedIn Caption (~230 words)

Last month I sat in a procurement review at a bank. Eight vendors on the shortlist. Every one described their product as an "AI agent."

The first was a chatbot with an LLM wrapper. The second was a PDF summariser. The third was an RPA script someone in marketing relabelled "agentic."

Three vendors. Three products. Not one was an agent.

Gartner found that only ~130 out of thousands of "agentic AI" vendors are genuinely agentic. The rest are chatbots, copilots, and RPA tools with a new label.

They predict over 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end of 2027. Agent-washing is a named contributing factor.

Meanwhile, 84% of C-suite executives say they will increase agent spend. The money is flowing. The problem is where it lands.

An agent is not a chatbot that got promoted. An agent pursues a goal, decomposes it into sub-tasks, orchestrates across systems, handles exceptions, and maintains an audit trail. If it cannot do these things, it is not an agent — regardless of what the slide deck says.

I use a seven-question test in every evaluation. Goal decomposition. Cross-system orchestration. Exception handling. Audit trail. Human override. Persistence. Failure mode.

Ask all seven. In the room. With the engineers.

That is the test.

# Agent-Washing — Pinned First Comment

The seven-question agent-readiness test:

1. Goal decomposition — Can it break a business objective into sub-tasks without human prompting?
2. Cross-system orchestration — Does it act across core banking, CRM, compliance, and document systems?
3. Exception handling — Feed it an edge case. Does it reason or break?
4. Audit trail — Does every decision produce a traceable log?
5. Human override — Can compliance halt, reverse, or correct mid-workflow?
6. Persistence — Does it maintain context across sessions?
7. Failure mode — When it is wrong, does it flag uncertainty or present confident nonsense?

Gartner's data: only ~130 of thousands of vendors are genuinely agentic. 40% of agentic AI projects will be cancelled by end of 2027. 84% of C-suite leaders are increasing spend anyway.

The agentic AI market has an integrity problem. It is ESG-washing five years ago. The pattern is the same.

The firms that extract real value are not the ones that buy the fastest. They are the ones that evaluate the hardest.
