Three reasons AI rollouts stall in banks

No governance from day one

Risk, compliance, and Sharia governance are bolted on after the POC — leading to months of rework when the regulator asks questions.

Vendor overload, no strategy

HUMAIN, AWS, Azure, OCI, GCP — every hyperscaler is pitching. Without a vendor-neutral architecture, banks buy platforms they never operationalise.

Consultants who leave decks, not systems

Big-firm teams hand over a 200-slide playbook and exit. Nobody stays to build, integrate, and drive adoption across the front line.
