# Part 2 — LinkedIn Caption (~220 words)

**"AI replaces the analyst." Two versions of this misconception. Both wrong.**

Version A: "Automate prospectus review. Deploy the model, reduce headcount by 60%, and clear the IPO backlog."

Version B: "AI can't be trusted with listing decisions. Too much liability. We'll wait."

The first misunderstands where the analyst's value lives. The second misunderstands what the technology does.

A listing analyst reads a 200-page prospectus, cross-references 47 disclosure requirements, and drafts a completeness report. Three days per filing. Twelve applications queued. The backlog grows.

But the analyst's value isn't in reading 200 pages. It's in the questions that come after: Is this management team credible? Does this business model survive a rate cycle?

The first principle: AI writes the first draft. Humans own the listing decision.

I designed a prospectus review workflow for an exchange context. The agent extracts, cross-references, and flags gaps. The analyst reviews in 45 minutes — focusing on judgment, not checklists. Throughput increases 4x. Headcount doesn't decrease.

The misconception leads to two dead ends. The principle unlocks a third path: the analyst's role elevates, the exchange's capacity multiplies, and the listing decision stays exactly where it should.
