# Part 3 — LinkedIn Caption (~220 words)

**"AI governance is a compliance exercise." That's the most dangerous misconception in asset management AI today.**

An asset manager deploys an AI agent to generate the daily Chief Investment Officer risk report. Factor exposures, sector tilts, VaR exceptions — synthesised into a narrative summary every morning.

One morning, the report includes a $40 million equity position that doesn't exist. The AI hallucinated it. Nobody catches it for three weeks.

When the post-mortem happens, the first question is: "Where was the governance?"

The answer: a 40-page PDF in the compliance folder. Approved by the board. Last reviewed in Q1. It governed nothing.

The first principle: governance is architecture, not paperwork.

Guardrails are code that runs at inference time. Safety guardrails that block data exfiltration. Quality guardrails that cross-validate every position against the portfolio. Compliance guardrails that log every query — the hash, not the content — for the audit trail that's coming.

SR 11-7 was rescinded in April 2026. The EU AI Act high-risk deadline is August 2026. SDAIA's Responsible AI Policy just opened for consultation. The regulatory landscape is shifting. The firms that survive the audits are the ones building governance into their architecture now.
