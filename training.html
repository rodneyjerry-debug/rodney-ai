6-week Adoption Architecture Programme

6 weeks · Senior advisor-led · Delivery-ready output

We design the enterprise-wide AI adoption strategy so your organisation can move from pockets of AI to pervasive, governed usage. You receive a blueprint your internal team or SI partner can execute immediately.

  1. AI Readiness Assessment — maturity scoring across business units, persona mapping, friction analysis
  2. Adoption Architecture — rollout waves by persona tier, adoption KPIs, tooling landscape (Anthropic, Google, Microsoft Copilot)
  3. Governance Guardrails — usage policies per user tier, data classification rules, escalation paths
  4. Champions Network Design — identify, recruit, and enable 50–100 internal AI champions across the organisation
  5. Change & Communications Playbook — messaging by audience, training curriculum, measurement framework
  6. Board Reporting Pack — adoption dashboard, risk register, value-realisation metrics
